Our Philosophy

We believe the needs and desires of the player dictate the path to better golf and more enjoyment. We believe the body is a tool. Each of our bodies are capable of performing different movements; through the use of body mechanics, we look to build the most powerful and yet efficient golf swing. Repetition is key, so good practice habits and drills are used to achieve consistency.  How to practice has become a priority in all facets of my teaching. Setting goals is important whether based on performance or enjoyment to measure progress for all students. We respect each individual and their journey towards achieving their goals
